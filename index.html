Save GrizzlyJohn to your Home Screen

Then it works like an app. No hunting for a link, no remembering what a browser tab is doing, no technology scavenger hunt.

Which phone are you using?

iPhone: three taps and you’re done.

Use Safari for the easiest setup.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari. It looks like a square with an arrow pointing up.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the upper-right corner.

Android: let Chrome do the work.

If the big green install button appears, use that. Otherwise follow the three steps below.

  1. Tap the ⋮ three-dot menu in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install or Add to confirm.

Once GrizzlyJohn is installed, this screen retires too.

ABOUT THIS APP

GrizzlyJohn

A little bit AA wisdom, a little bit wilderness adventure, a little bit “what weird thing should I learn today?” and a whole lot of John.

Built for a man who looks suspiciously like Santa Claus, has the heart of a teddy bear, the life experience of a very well-traveled grizzly, and enough self-awareness to know when even lunch might be a questionable life choice.

There’s no fixing you in here. After nearly 25 years of sobriety, you’ve probably learned a thing or two already.

Just keep wandering, keep laughing, keep thinking, keep noticing the good stuff.

🐻 Stay grounded. Stay curious. Behave-ish.
