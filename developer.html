Privacy built in

Collection only begins when John opens Settings, enters the separate sharing code and opts in. He can switch it off in Settings at any time. The dashboard receives anonymous counts, broad feature names, dates and hours. Journal writing, feelings, sleep data, medication details and locations are never sent.

Past collected counts remain on Netlify after opting out. This dashboard never reads or modifies John's personal app records or encrypted backups.
